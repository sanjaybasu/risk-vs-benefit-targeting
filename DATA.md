## Downloaded but not loaded

- **crash2**: public file (hbiostat.org) has no treatment allocation column; not usable as an RCT
- **upworthy**: aggregated clicks per headline arm; no viewer covariates; fits `choose`, not targeting
- **llmrouterbench**: JSON per model per benchmark; layout check pending
- **lmarena**: pairwise human preferences only; cannot give every model's outcome per prompt

## Terms that restrict use

- Criteo uplift (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and the Oregon public-use files (NBER click-through: no commercial use, no re-identification, cite) are evaluation-only. No model that Waymark sells is trained on them.
- Hillstrom, NSW, IHDP, RouterBench data, and SWE-bench experiments state no license; treat as research use only until counsel reads them.
- Camden (CC0), Michigan social pressure (CC0), Illinois (CC0), and IST (ODC-By, attribution) permit commercial use.

The detectable gap is the smallest difference, in outcome standard deviations, between two rankings' mean effect among the top 20% that the test split detects with 80% power at a two-sided 0.05 level. It treats the two selected sets as independent, so it is conservative.
